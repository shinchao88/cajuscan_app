CajuScan is a Flutter app for Android that simplifies expense tracking in Portugal. Scan the ATCUD QR code on an invoice to extract its transaction details and send them to the Cashew personal finance app.

Features:
- Scan invoice QR codes using your camera, PDF files, or gallery images.
- Extract invoice amounts, dates, merchant tax IDs, and other available data.
- Send invoice details to Cashew, with optional tax metadata in transaction notes.
- Save merchant profiles and suggest categories and destination accounts from your local history.
- Manage merchants, categories, and accounts; import category and account data from Cashew.
- Back up and restore app data using JSON files.

Privacy-focused: data is processed locally, with no tracking, ads, or external servers.
